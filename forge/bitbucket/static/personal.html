Link personal identity

In OpenReview Settings → Personal setup, generate a personal identity code for this connection. Paste it here while signed in as the account that authors your pull requests. This verifies the account you are signed in as and its read access to the selected repository. OpenReview stores your short-lived token encrypted for at most one hour and rechecks access before cross-repository review or publication. No workspace-owner role is needed. Disconnect in OpenReview or confirm again after expiry.
